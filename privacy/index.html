Days Kept

Privacy Policy

Effective October 9, 2026

Short version: Days Kept does not collect, store or share any of your data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What the app stores

The habits you create — their names, emoji, colors, start dates and order — are saved only on your iPhone or iPad, in storage shared between the app and its widgets. This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us or to anyone else.

What we collect

Nothing. Days Kept has no accounts or sign-up, and no analytics, advertising or tracking. It contains no third-party code that collects data, and it makes no network requests.

Backups

If you use iCloud Backup or back up your device to a computer, your habits may be included in that backup as part of your device's normal backup, under Apple's privacy policy. We have no access to these backups.

Deleting your data

Delete a habit in the app to remove it. Delete the app to remove all of its data from your device.

Children

Days Kept does not collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, for example because a future version adds a feature that handles data, we will update this page and the effective date above before that version is released.
