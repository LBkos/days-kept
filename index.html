Support & FAQ

How do I add a widget?
  1. Touch and hold an empty area of your Home Screen until the apps jiggle.
  2. Tap Edit → Add Widget and search for Days Kept.
  3. Choose Habit Streak (one habit) or Habit List (several), pick a size and tap Add Widget.
How do I choose which habit a widget shows?

Touch and hold the widget, tap Edit Widget, then pick a habit. For the Habit List widget you can pick several habits, or leave it empty to show all habits in the same order as the app.

How are days counted?

Days Kept counts full 24-hour days since the start date and time you set. A habit started yesterday at 8 PM shows 1 day from today at 8 PM. Widgets update at exactly that moment.

How do I change the order of my habits?

Touch and hold a habit card and drag it to a new place, or tap Reorder to use drag handles. The sort button can also arrange habits once by longest streak, newest or name.

I slipped. How do I start over?

Open the habit and tap Restart, or touch and hold its card and choose Restart from Now. The counter goes back to 0 and starts again from this moment.

Can I change the start date of a habit?

Yes. Open the habit and tap Edit. You can set any date and time in the past.

Will my habits sync to my other devices or be backed up?

Habits are stored only on your device. They're included in your regular iPhone or iPad backups, but they don't sync between devices. Deleting the app deletes your habits.
